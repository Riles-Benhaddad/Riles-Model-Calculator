Advanced parameters
g = 0 gives the plain block model. s = 1 (or r = 0) means no soft-tissue adjustment. Per §24–25 of the paper, keep g and r only if they improve blind prediction.
Length: L = (S/α)1/p · (C/C0)−g/p (§7). Mass: M = a · Lb · sr (§14), with a = 0.001 and b = 3.5 as in the original calculator.
Family constants come from the spreadsheet's Equations & Notes sheet. Every family except Tyrannosauridae uses a C3–C5 block.
If a specimen falls outside ±15% of the family, the model may not apply.
